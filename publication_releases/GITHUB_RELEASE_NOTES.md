# Paper 1 reproducibility release v1.0.0

Frozen publication release for:

**Whether model fairness is visible at pathway level depends on how often access is constrained**

This release contains the exact Article 1 v11 analysis runner, the frozen healthcare-equity implementation used by the runner, reproducibility metadata, publication CSV outputs and figures.

## Important

- Third-party source datasets are **not** redistributed.
- Download UCI Cleveland and GRID3 from their official providers.
- Verify their SHA-256 hashes before claiming exact reproduction.
- Geographic replications are Monte Carlo case-location realizations, not independent patients.
- The 40 km/h geodesic travel-time quantity is an optimistic accessibility proxy, not observed road-network travel time.
